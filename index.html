Meet Sowntharya, founder of SISA

SISA was built on a simple idea — makeup that photographs beautifully and still feels like you by the end of a long function day. From the first Haldi to the Reception night, every look is tailored to your skin, your outfit and your event.

With a steady hand for both HD and airbrush finishes, Sowntharya works across bridal, bridesmaid, party and groom makeup, pairing every look with hairstyling and saree or shawl draping for a complete, camera-ready finish.
